About Us

Your Trusted Cleaning Partner Since 2021

Anambewe Cleaning Service and Maidservant was established in 2021 in Lilongwe and was registered under Business Registration Act (No.12 of 2012) in Malawi Business.

We are passionate about providing flexible and customized services where every customer is treated equally no matter how large or small the contract value. We ensure that every contract is maintained at a consistent level of quality through regular spot checks and scheduled visits.

Our areas of expertise include Industrial, Commercial, Corporate Office, Hospital and Residential Cleaning. We aim to provide quality service as we believe that a clean and healthy environment is beneficial to one's physical and mental health.
